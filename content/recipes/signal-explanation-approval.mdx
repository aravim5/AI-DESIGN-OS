## Purpose

Use this recipe when the system detects an issue, risk, or opportunity and a human must explicitly authorize the next step. This is especially valuable for compliance-sensitive, financially material, or customer-impacting workflows.

This is the right recipe for any flow where the system should surface, explain, and then *wait* — not act.

## Workflow Shape

```
Signal → Triage Summary → Explanation → Approval Checkpoint → Execute or Route
```

## Primary Patterns

1. **Proactive Signal** — system-initiated detection and surfacing
2. **Evidence-Backed Answer** — explanation with traceable rationale
3. **Approval Checkpoint** — explicit human authorization gate
4. **Audit / History View** — record of what was shown, approved, and executed

## Inputs Required

- Monitored entity, workflow, or threshold
- Signal logic and triggering criteria
- Supporting evidence bundle
- Reviewer identity and authorization scope
- Reversibility status of the proposed action

## Outputs

- Signal with explanation
- Approval or rejection, with reviewer identity
- Action executed or routed, with audit record

## Autonomy Level

**Level 4 — Execute with Approval.** The system detects, explains, and prepares — but takes no action until the human explicitly authorizes. The pause point is mandatory.

## Trust Requirements

- Signal must be explainable — user should know what triggered it
- Evidence must be sufficient for informed approval — not just a summary
- Approval must be genuinely consequential — not a formality
- Audit record must capture what was shown, what was approved, and what ran
- Workflow must preserve state through rejection and re-submission

## Example Scenario

A KYC review system detects a profile needing attention. The system surfaces the signal, summarizes the triggering evidence (recent transactions, document expiry, unresolved flags), and queues the case for supervisor sign-off. The supervisor approves or requests investigation. The action does not proceed until authorization is given.

## Modality and Surface Variants

- **Notification + sidebar** — signal delivered as notification, expanded in sidebar for review
- **Workspace** — full case detail before approval decision
- **Email or async channel** — for batched approvals in lower-urgency flows

## Failure Modes

- Signal is noisy or confidence is misread as certainty — reviewer approves without real understanding
- Approval step lacks sufficient evidence for informed judgment
- Workflow resumes without durable state after rejection or edit
- Multiple reviewers approve the same item in parallel — ownership unclear
